## Full description

WhereIP is a small, free, and open-source network utility.

Open the app to see your public IPv4 or IPv6 address, country, approximate
city and region, network organization, ASN, timezone, and the provider that
returned the result. Available fields depend on the selected provider.

Privacy is part of the interface:

- no WhereIP account or backend;
- no ads or analytics;
- no GPS permission;
- a clear disclosure before the first request;
- direct links to every provider's privacy policy and terms;
- local caching and a refresh cooldown to avoid unnecessary requests; and
- complete source code available on GitHub.

IP location is approximate and can represent a VPN, carrier gateway, business
network, or nearby city. WhereIP never presents it as the device's precise
physical location.

## Review notes

The first-run disclosure must be acknowledged before any provider request is
made. The application needs internet access for its primary function. It does
not require a login, subscription, payment, or special test account.

The IP result may differ between the reviewer's browser and device because it
describes the public egress network.
